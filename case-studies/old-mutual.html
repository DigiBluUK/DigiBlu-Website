Old Mutual

Overview

Old Mutual is a leading financial services provider offering insurance, investment and banking solutions. Facing rapid market change, rising customer expectations, growing competition and an increasingly complex regulatory environment, they turned to Robotic Process Automation to improve efficiency and stay competitive.

The problem

Operational costs were climbing alongside regulatory demands, while manual, outdated processes held efficiency back. Customers faced longer waits and inconsistent service, and regulatory complexity added further administrative burden - making it harder to compete on fast, reliable service.

What we did

We began with an in-depth pilot, assessing existing processes to identify the highest-impact automation opportunities. From there we worked with Old Mutual to establish a group-level operating model for managing a scalable digital workforce, targeting core servicing and administrative processes while laying the foundation for future automation. A comprehensive training programme equipped Old Mutual’s own employees to manage and scale the initiative, making the change sustainable rather than dependent on us.

Outcome

Old Mutual automated 64 processes across 262 production deployments, saving over 150,000 hours of manual work and freeing staff for more strategic tasks. Return on investment exceeded 200%, well beyond the norm for comparable projects. Half of all customer cases are now processed the same day, with average processing time cut in half.

Experienced practitioners with client, technology, and consultancy backgrounds, working as pragmatic, technology-agnostic partners focused on speed to value.
