Education · Process Excellence

Streamlining HR and payroll integration through automation.

Northwest University

37,000+Undergraduate students
FewerPayroll data errors
ReducedTemporary staff costs

Overview

Northwest University is one of South Africa’s largest universities, serving over 37,000 undergraduates across three campuses with nearly 5,000 academic and support staff. Rising student numbers alongside falling government allocations had forced hiring freezes, leaving HR administration and Finance Payroll to do more with less.

The problem

Data moved between HR and Payroll through email-driven processes and manually attached forms. The approach was prone to errors, duplication and delays, creating bottlenecks in payroll processing, hurting employee satisfaction and driving unnecessary cost through manual checks, rework and temporary staff at peak periods.

What we did

We ran an in-depth opportunity assessment to understand the pain points, then used Blue Prism Cloud and Jotform to design and implement automation replacing the manual email and forms process. The solution automates data extraction, validation and integration with the university’s core HR and payroll systems, and triggers alerts on any discrepancy so errors surface early.

Outcome

Removing manual entry and pre/post quality checks significantly reduced data errors and shortened payroll processing, so staff are paid faster. Integrating the two functions improved collaboration between teams, and the university needed far fewer temporary staff at peak. Success has prompted interest in extending automation further, with DigiBlu now designing an operating model for managing the National Student Financial Aid Scheme.
