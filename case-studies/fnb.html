Overview

First National Bank is one of South Africa’s premier financial institutions, operating across several African countries since 1838 and known for using technology to improve the banking experience.

The problem

Despite significant investment in intelligent automation, FNB depended heavily on external consultants because internal resources and expertise were limited. To build its own capability, the bank wanted a Centre of Excellence for Robotic Process Automation.

What we did

After a successful pilot automating a high-volume account opening process, we set up the CoE: engaging stakeholders to assess capability and define objectives and KPIs, adapting the Blue Prism Robotic Operating Model using DigiBlu’s Framework for Virtual Operations, and delivering a CoE blueprint covering service, operating and governance models. We wrote the policies and processes for demand, delivery and operations management, and trained staff as automation engineers, process controllers and automation managers.

Outcome

The new team automated account opening across 142 screens of the core banking system - 50,000 accounts a month, five bots, and processing time down from 10 minutes to 3, releasing 12 full-time equivalents. Fourteen candidates completed DigiBlu’s training boot camp with over 80% passing the Blue Prism Developer accreditation, leaving FNB able to sustain and expand automation itself.

Experienced practitioners with client, technology, and consultancy backgrounds, working as pragmatic, technology-agnostic partners focused on speed to value.
