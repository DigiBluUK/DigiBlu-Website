## Design system

CSS custom properties on `:root`, redefined under `:root[data-theme="light"]` and `@media (prefers-color-scheme: light)`:

- `--ink-rgb`, `--text` — flip between white-on-black and near-black-on-white. Individual elements override `--ink-rgb`/`--text` locally when they sit on a fixed-color surface regardless of theme (hero, accent cards, blue buttons, the Case Studies background band) — grep `--ink-rgb: 255, 255, 255` to find all of them.
- `--bg`, `--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--field-bg` — theme-aware surface tones.
- `--blue` (`#1d6ef5`) — the accent colour for anything that has to be a single colour: text, links, hover tints, focus rings, the values' hover numeral.
- `--brand-fill` — **the primary fill since 9 Sep 2026**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #1b4fd6 0%, #0a1f6e 52%, #050b28 100%)`, the blue-to-navy-to-near-black sweep the service cards and the open team card carry, promoted on request to the one brand surface site-wide ("so brand colour is in sync and this becomes a primary colour of use"). Every surface that was flat `--blue` uses it: the Get in touch button, the scroll-to-top disc, the case-study stat pills, the reader's selected row and current page button, the team strip's arrows, open slice and mobile + disc, the service dialog's CTA, the contact form's next/submit/done buttons and completed step numbers, and the skip link. Borders that matched the flat blue went transparent so the dark end of the sweep shows no blue rim, and the one hover that swapped in a lighter blue now brightens the gradient with `filter`. Same value in both themes on purpose, like the hero; ink on it is pinned white and clears AA at every size (white on the lightest stop is ~6.5:1). `.blog-art.a2` is the same sweep as a literal, kept separate because the art panels are a set of four.
- `--brand-ink-1/-2/-3` and `--brand-ink` — **the same sweep for accents** (9 Sep 2026, "that colour flows through all the way ... our values when you hover, the header, the geometric icon"): things that sit ON the page rather than under white ink. In light theme the three tokens are the fill's own stops, so accent and surface are literally one colour. **In dark theme they are the family's bright end** (`#6aa4ff`, `#2f7bff`, `#1d6ef5`): the fill's navy end is 1.4:1 on black and even its brand-blue start only 3.1:1, so the fill sweep cannot be text there; the bright sweep measures 6.6, 5.4 and 4.56:1, every stop clear of AA for the 12px label. Consumers: the "Our values" label (gradient text, `width: max-content` so the sweep crosses the words), the values' hover and focus numeral (gradient text, applied in the hover state only because the phone column's collapsed numerals are translucent and a permanent gradient would bleed through, and transitioned on the way in only or the numeral flashes out) and hairline (`border-image`), the client quote's left rule, the contact form's success tick (its own `#tickBrand` gradient) and **the About mark** - the 78 mark dots are emitted a second time by `dots.cjs` in a `<g class="mk-lit">` filled from `#geoBrand`, whose opacity is the `--mark-lit` the geo IIFE already writes; the ink dots underneath stay `--text`. Colour tokens rather than a gradient alone so SVG `<stop>`s can read them (`.gb1/.gb2/.gb3`). What stays flat `--blue`: focus rings, the checkbox `accent-color` and the quote rule's fallback colour under its border-image - indicators and fallbacks, not accents.
- `--section-y` (72px desktop / 56px ≤900px), `--head-gap` (48px / 36px) - **the vertical rhythm.** Every section pads from `--section-y`; every heading's trailing margin is `--head-gap`. Boundary gap between any two adjacent sections should measure `2 × --section-y`. If you add or restructure a section, give it `padding: var(--section-y) var(--gutter)` directly - don't hand-roll a padding value, and don't put that padding on an inner wrapper only (this exact mistake broke the rhythm twice this session, see Case Studies in `sections.md`).
- `--maxw` (1500px), `--gutter` (24px) — content width and page margin.
- `--page-ink-rgb` / `--page-text` — **escape hatch, never overridden below `:root`.** `.hero` and `.cases` pin their own white ink so text stays legible on their artwork. Anything nested inside them that is visually part of the *page* rather than that surface must opt back out via `var(--page-ink-rgb)`, or it inherits white ink while taking a theme-aware background. That exact mismatch made the mobile menu render white-on-white (contrast 1.0, completely unreadable) in light mode on every viewport ≤900px. `.mobile-menu` is the current consumer.
- `--ink-a-faint` / `-low` / `-mid` / `-high` / `-aa` — alpha steps for de-emphasised text, **higher in light mode**. The dark values (0.40/0.45/0.50/0.55/0.58) are the originals and are unchanged; the light overrides (0.60/0.62/0.64/0.66/0.66) exist because the same alpha on white measured only 2.6–4.3:1. Use these tokens rather than a literal `rgba(var(--ink-rgb), 0.5)` for any muted text, or it will fail AA in light mode. Anything already at 0.6+ clears 4.5:1 in both themes and is left literal.
- `color-scheme` is declared on `:root` in all three theme states (dark default, explicit light, OS-preference light). It is what tells the browser which way round to paint its own native UI - without it the UA assumes a light page and paints a pale scrollbar over the dark site, and it is the only thing keeping the native bar in the right key in browsers that ignore the custom scrollbar rules.
- **Scrollbars are styled site-wide**, not per-container: a `*::-webkit-scrollbar` rule plus `scrollbar-width`/`-color` on `html` for Firefox, all resolved through `--ink-rgb` so both themes need one rule. `border: 3px solid transparent` + `background-clip: content-box` is what insets the thumb inside its 10px gutter. The universal selector is deliberate so containers added later are covered; `::-webkit-scrollbar` does not inherit the way `scrollbar-color` does, so it cannot just be set on `html`. Confirm it applied by measuring the rendered gutter (`innerWidth - documentElement.clientWidth` = 10, against a 15-17px Windows default), not by reading the rule back.
- Theme toggle button (`#themeToggle`) persists an explicit choice to `localStorage['digiblu-theme']`; absent a stored choice, `prefers-color-scheme` decides. A small inline `<script>` in `<head>` (and re-emitted before `<body>` in the artifact build) stamps `data-theme` before first paint to avoid a flash.
