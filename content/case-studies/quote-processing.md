## Overview

Our client is a large national manufacturer and provider of mobility solutions, handling a significant volume of supplier quotations as part of its day-to-day operations. Each quotation had to have its information extracted, interpreted and compared against the original product specification before the case could be resolved, and with much of that work relying on manual review the client saw an opportunity to use AI and automation to build a faster, more scalable process.

## The problem

Supplier quotations vary considerably in format, structure and content, so traditional rules-based automation could not be applied across the whole process. Teams reviewed each quotation by hand, identified the relevant products and specifications, compared them against the original requirements and decided the outcome. That level of manual intervention increased processing effort and limited how far the operation could scale, while the complexity of some quotations meant a fully automated approach would not suit every case. The client needed a solution that combined intelligent document interpretation and automated decision-making with controlled human intervention where required.

## What we did

DigiBlu designed and delivered an intelligent quote-processing solution that combines Generative AI, intelligent document processing, workflow automation and human-in-the-loop decision making in a single end-to-end process. It extracts the relevant information from each incoming supplier quotation, matches the quoted products against their original specifications, and applies business rules and defined guidelines to determine whether the case can be resolved automatically or needs further review. Where an exception is identified, the case is routed to a purpose-built reconciliation application that gives users a fast, controlled way to review and resolve the more complex cases. AI-driven interpretation sits alongside deterministic business rules, enterprise integrations and operational reporting, so the solution stays controlled and auditable while benefiting from the flexibility of Generative AI. DigiBlu continues to support it as a managed service, with an operational framework for monitoring, maintaining and continuously improving the automation in production.

## Outcome

A predominantly manual quote-handling process is now a scalable intelligent automation capability. Routine cases progress automatically within defined business rules, reducing the volume of manual review and letting operational teams focus on the exceptions and cases that genuinely require human judgement. The solution also creates a rich operational data source, giving greater visibility of quote processing and supporting the identification of further opportunities for optimisation and continuous improvement. By integrating AI, automation, data and people in a single process, the project shows how Generative AI can be deployed in a controlled enterprise environment to deliver practical operational benefits while retaining appropriate human oversight.
